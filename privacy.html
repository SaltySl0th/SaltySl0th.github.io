Moult Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Moult is an app that teaches Python programming. It is made by Filament and Found Limited, a New Zealand company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you use Moult on Android. Moult is designed to be suitable for children as well as adults.

The short version

  • Moult has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress and the code you write are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • Moult shows ads from Google AdMob. They are set up for children: ads are rated G (suitable for all ages), they are never personalised, and the app does not use your device's advertising ID.
  • To show ads, Google receives some technical information described below.

Information stored on your device

Moult saves your level progress, experience points, daily challenge results, settings, and the Python code you write. This is stored inside the app on your device only. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling Moult deletes it. Choosing "Reset progress" in the app clears your progress.

The Python code you write runs on your own device. If a program you write connects to a website, that connection is made by your program to that website, not to us.

Ads and Google AdMob

Moult is free and is paid for by ads. A full-screen ad is shown after every third new level you complete and after each daily task, and you can choose to watch an ad to unlock a hint. Ads need an internet connection.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We tell Google that Moult is directed to children and that its users may be under the age of consent, and we limit ads to the G content rating. When Moult is set up this way, Google shows only non-personalised ads, which are based on things like the app being used rather than on your interests or history.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in Moult automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate the general area your device is in (approximate location).
  • Interactions with ads, such as when an ad is shown, tapped or closed, and app launches.
  • Diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform, such as loading times.
  • The app set ID, an identifier for the apps from one developer on your device. Moult removes access to the Android advertising ID, so it is not collected.

This information is encrypted in transit. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell any information. Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Moult has no chat, no sharing, no accounts and no way to send us personal information from inside the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of Moult, please contact us using the details below.

Your choices

  • You can delete everything Moult stores on your device by uninstalling the app.
  • You can reset your device's app set ID and other Google settings from your Android settings and at My Ad Center.
  • Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and similar laws, you can ask us what information we hold about you. Because Moult does not send us personal information, we normally hold none.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Moult handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Using Moult is also covered by our Terms of Use.
